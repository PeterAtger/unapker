# Unapker privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Unapker does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- It has no internet permission and never connects to a network.
- It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or accounts.
- Files you open are copied to the app's private cache only while they install, then deleted.
- If a bundle includes game data (OBB) that Android doesn't let Unapker place directly, Unapker saves it to `Download/Unapker` on your phone so you can move it.

Unapker asks for one special permission, "Install unknown apps", because installing the bundles you open is the only thing it does.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/PeterAtger/unapker/issues
